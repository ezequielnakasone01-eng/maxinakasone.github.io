[PIEL] with visible pores, deep horizontal forehead lines, pronounced crow's feet, deep nasolabial folds, puffy under-eye bags, age spots on the temples and the backs of his hands, slightly loose skin on the neck, visible veins on the hands. [ROSTRO], [PELO], [BARBA], [CEJAS], [OJOS/GAFAS], [EXPRESIÓN]. Wearing [ROPA COMPLETA], [RELOJ], a gold wedding band. On his feet, [CALZADO].
- Mujer: misma estructura ("woman"), con "X m tall barefoot and Y m in her heels".
- Si sale joven: reforzar papada, venas, canas; sacar "lean/fit".
- Nunca "olive skin" en personajes tejidos o animados (sale verde). En reales, preferir "warm tan / sun-tanned Mediterranean skin".
- Para el "después" con SAYU: misma imagen + fotos del producto como ingrediente.
